## Hardware reality (do not fake)

* **No PMIC ADC.** Pi 3B+ has no `vcgencmd pmic_read_adc`; per-rail power
  telemetry is impossible. The hardening package already reports this honestly
  via `caps["pmic_adc"]` and emits `per-rail power unavailable on this board`.
* **No usable I2C.** Only `/dev/i2c-2` is present, which is the HDMI DDC bus.
  `i2c-1` (the GPIO header bus the Waveshare UPS HAT needs) is **not enabled**,
  and `i2cdetect` is not installed. The UPS HAT cannot currently be read.
* **SPI present**: `/dev/spidev0.0`, `/dev/spidev0.1`.
* `vcgencmd get_throttled = 0x80000` — bit 19, *soft temperature limit has
  occurred* (sticky). No undervoltage, no current throttling.

## Current behaviour

Every 60 s: `Sync HTTP error: 401 Unauthorized` on
`/api/v1/measurements/batch`, and `Stored | A: 0.0W B: 0.0W C: 0.0W` every
~50 s. It is buffering locally and retrying — the designed behaviour, no data
loss.

**The 401 is correct and expected.** Node 03 is `registered`
(`may_upload = 0`). Installing its credential would change 401 to 403; it would
still, correctly, not upload. There is nothing to unblock without a
commissioning decision.
